# Virello AI — Your AI Growth Manager

**🌐 Website:** https://virelloai.online

Virello is an AI-powered social media growth platform. Paste your post, choose your goal, set an optional budget — and Virello's AI handles analysis, audience strategy, growth optimization, campaign management and reporting.

## What it does

- **AI Content Analysis** — every post scored for hook strength, clarity and viral potential
- **Smart Audience Matching** — content matched with genuinely interested audiences across 248 countries
- **Organic Growth Engine** — free AI-driven organic growth ($0 budget)
- **AI Paid Boost** — budget-paced campaigns inside limits you approve
- **Real-Time Analytics** — reach, engagement, follower growth, plain-language reports
- **Fraud & Safety Protection** — no bots, no fake engagement, ever

## Principles

- ✅ Real growth from real people — never bots, never fake engagement
- ✅ No password sharing — secure, authorized connections only
- ✅ You approve the budget and targeting — AI never spends beyond it
- ✅ Google Play Billing for digital purchases
