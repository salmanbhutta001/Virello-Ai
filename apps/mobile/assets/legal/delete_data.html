Data Deletion

Virello · Last updated: October 5, 2026

You can delete your Virello account and all associated data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open the Virello app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account at the bottom of the Settings screen.
  4. Confirm the deletion in the dialog.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (email, name, profile photo, language preferences).
  • Your campaigns, posts, scripts, captions, templates, and uploaded media.
  • Your saved products, notifications, and support conversations.
  • Your credits, subscriptions, and transaction history.

What is retained

  • Anonymized aggregate analytics (e.g. daily active-user counts) that cannot be linked back to you.
  • Records we are legally required to keep (e.g. tax or fraud-prevention records), kept only as long as the law requires.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address associated with your account at privacy@virello.com with the subject "Delete my account". We will verify your identity and delete your data within 30 days.
